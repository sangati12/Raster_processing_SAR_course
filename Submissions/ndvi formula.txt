I understand the NDVI formula:

NDVI = (Near-Infrared − Red) ÷ (Near-Infrared + Red)

The numerator is calculated by subtracting the Red reflectance value from the Near-Infrared (NIR) reflectance value.

The denominator is calculated by adding the Near-Infrared (NIR) and Red reflectance values.


NDVI Interpretation Range
NDVI Value	Interpretation
-1 to 0	Water, bare surfaces, or non-vegetated areas
0 to 0.2	Bare soil, rock, or very sparse vegetation
0.2 to 0.4	Sparse or moderate vegetation
0.4 to 0.6	Moderate to healthy vegetation
0.6 to 0.8	Healthy, dense vegetation
0.8 to 1.0	Very dense and highly healthy vegetation

These ranges provide a general interpretation of vegetation condition. Higher positive NDVI values generally indicate healthier and denser vegetation, while values near zero or below zero indicate little or no vegetation.
